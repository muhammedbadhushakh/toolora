Frequently Asked Questions

What does it check?

Ten on-page factors: title tag, meta description, H1 heading, canonical tag, mobile viewport, image alt text, Open Graph tags, robots meta, HTTPS, and overall content length.

Does a perfect score mean the page will rank well?

No — this checks on-page technical basics only. Rankings also depend on off-page factors like backlinks, content quality and competition, which this tool doesn't measure.

Why did a check fail even though the page looks fine?

Checks are based on the page's raw HTML at the time of the scan — pages that build their title, headings or meta tags with JavaScript after load may show a false "missing" result here.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it works on any modern desktop or mobile browser.
