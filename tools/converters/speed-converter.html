Frequently Asked Questions

Which speed units are supported?

Meters per second (m/s), kilometers per hour (km/h), miles per hour (mph), knots and feet per second (ft/s) — covering everyday, aviation and marine use.

How is a knot defined here?

As 1 nautical mile per hour (1.852 km/h), the standard used in aviation and maritime navigation.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — conversions are calculated locally in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it's fully responsive on phones and tablets as well as desktop.
