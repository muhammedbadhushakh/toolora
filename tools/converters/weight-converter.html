Frequently Asked Questions

Which weight units are supported?

Kilograms, grams, milligrams, metric tonnes, pounds and ounces — covering both metric and imperial/US units.

Is this weight or mass?

Like most everyday converters, it treats "weight" and "mass" the same way (1 kg = 2.20462 lb), which is accurate for normal Earth-surface use.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — the conversion is calculated locally in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it's fully responsive on phones and tablets as well as desktop.
