How to use Number to Words

  1. Type a number into the input box.
  2. Click convert to spell it out in words.
  3. Copy the result with one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

What numbering system does this use?

The international (Western) system — thousand, million, billion, trillion — rather than the Indian lakh/crore system.

What's the largest number it can spell out?

Up to the trillions. Numbers larger than that fall outside the tool's word groups and won't convert correctly.

Can I convert negative numbers?

Yes — negative values are prefixed with "negative" in the output.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No, the conversion runs entirely in your browser.
