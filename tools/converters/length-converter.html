How to use Length Converter

  1. Pick the unit you're converting from and to (meters, km, cm, mm, miles, yards, feet or inches).
  2. Enter the value — the result updates instantly.
  3. Copy the converted figure with one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which length units are supported?

Metric: meters, kilometers, centimeters, millimeters. Imperial: miles, yards, feet and inches.

How precise is the conversion?

It uses exact standard conversion factors (for example, 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly), so results are as accurate as your input value.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — the math runs locally in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it's fully responsive on phones and tablets as well as desktop.
