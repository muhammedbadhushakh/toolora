Frequently Asked Questions

Which currencies are supported?

USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, AUD, CAD, CNY, AED and SGD.

Are these live exchange rates?

No — this tool uses fixed reference rates that are updated periodically, not real-time market data. For time-sensitive transfers or trading, check a live-rate source such as your bank or a financial data provider.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The conversion is calculated locally in your browser — nothing you enter is uploaded.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it's fully responsive on phones and tablets as well as desktop.
