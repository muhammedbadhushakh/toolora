Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Resizer free to use?

Yes, Image Resizer is completely free with no watermark or sign-up.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image Resizer processes images locally in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

What image formats does Image Resizer support?

Image Resizer supports common formats like JPG, PNG, and WebP.

Does Image Resizer reduce image quality?

Image Resizer is designed to preserve as much quality as possible for the operation it performs; some adjustments (like compression) involve an inherent size-vs-quality trade-off that you control.
