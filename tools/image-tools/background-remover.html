How to use Background Remover

  1. Upload the image you want to edit.
  2. Background Remover processes it directly in your browser using the Canvas API.
  3. Preview the result and download the edited image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Background Remover free to use?

Yes, Background Remover is completely free with no watermark or sign-up.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Background Remover processes images locally in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

What image formats does Background Remover support?

Background Remover supports common formats like JPG, PNG, and WebP.

Does Background Remover reduce image quality?

Background Remover is designed to preserve as much quality as possible for the operation it performs; some adjustments (like compression) involve an inherent size-vs-quality trade-off that you control.
