How to use Image Watermark

  1. Upload the image you want to edit.
  2. Image Watermark processes it directly in your browser using the Canvas API.
  3. Preview the result and download the edited image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Watermark free to use?

Yes, Image Watermark is completely free with no watermark or sign-up.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image Watermark processes images locally in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

What image formats does Image Watermark support?

Image Watermark supports common formats like JPG, PNG, and WebP.

Does Image Watermark reduce image quality?

Image Watermark is designed to preserve as much quality as possible for the operation it performs; some adjustments (like compression) involve an inherent size-vs-quality trade-off that you control.
