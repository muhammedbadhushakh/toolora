How to use Image Upscaler

  1. Upload the image you want to edit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Upscaler free to use?

Yes, Image Upscaler is completely free with no watermark or sign-up.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image Upscaler processes images locally in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

What image formats does Image Upscaler support?

Image Upscaler supports common formats like JPG, PNG, and WebP.

Does Image Upscaler reduce image quality?

Image Upscaler is designed to preserve as much quality as possible for the operation it performs; some adjustments (like compression) involve an inherent size-vs-quality trade-off that you control.
