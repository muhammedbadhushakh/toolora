Frequently Asked Questions

Is PNG to JPG free to use?

Yes, PNG to JPG is completely free with no watermark or sign-up.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. PNG to JPG processes images locally in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

What image formats does PNG to JPG support?

PNG to JPG supports common formats like JPG, PNG, and WebP.

Does PNG to JPG reduce image quality?

PNG to JPG is designed to preserve as much quality as possible for the operation it performs; some adjustments (like compression) involve an inherent size-vs-quality trade-off that you control.
