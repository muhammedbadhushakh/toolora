How to use Character Counter

  1. Paste or type your text into the box.
  2. Watch the live character, word and platform-limit counts update as you type.
  3. Trim your text until it fits the limit you need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which platform limits does it track?

X/Twitter (280 characters), SMS (160 characters) and a typical meta description limit (160 characters), alongside total characters, characters excluding spaces, and word count.

Does it count characters with or without spaces?

Both — you'll see separate figures for total characters and characters with spaces removed.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Everything is counted locally in your browser and never uploaded or saved.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it's fully responsive on phones and tablets.
