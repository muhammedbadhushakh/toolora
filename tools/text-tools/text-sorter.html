Sort lines alphabetically or numerically, ascending or descending.

How to use Text Sorter

  1. Paste your list into the box, one item per line.
  2. Choose a sort order — A→Z, Z→A, numeric ascending/descending, or by line length — and optionally remove duplicates.
  3. Click Sort and copy the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sort orders are available?

Alphabetical A→Z or Z→A, numeric ascending or descending, and sort by line length.

Can it remove duplicate lines while sorting?

Yes — tick "Also remove duplicates" to sort and de-duplicate your list in the same step.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No, sorting happens entirely in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it's fully responsive on phones and tablets.
