Frequently Asked Questions

How does it detect sentence boundaries?

It splits text on periods, question marks and exclamation points — so abbreviations (like "e.g." or "Dr.") can occasionally be counted as separate sentences.

Why does average words-per-sentence matter?

It's a quick proxy for readability — most style guides suggest keeping the average under 20 words for easy-to-read web and business writing.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No, everything is counted locally in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it's fully responsive on phones and tablets.
