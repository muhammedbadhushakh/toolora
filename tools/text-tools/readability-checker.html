How to use Readability Checker

  1. Paste or type the text you want to check.
  2. Click the check button to score it.
  3. Review the Flesch reading ease score, grade level and word/sentence/syllable counts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What formula does this use?

The Flesch Reading Ease and Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level formulas — standard readability metrics based on sentence length and syllable count per word.

How do I read the score?

Higher Flesch scores mean easier text: 90+ is roughly 5th-grade level, 60–70 is standard/plain English, and below 30 is difficult, graduate-level writing.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No — the analysis runs entirely in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it's fully responsive on phones and tablets.
