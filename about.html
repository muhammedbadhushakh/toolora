About Toolrua

Everyday tools, all in one place — free, fast, and built to respect your time.

What Toolrua is

Toolrua is a collection of small, focused web tools for the tasks people run into every day: converting a file, resizing an image, calculating a number, formatting some code, or cleaning up a block of text. Instead of hunting for a different app or paid subscription for each one-off task, Toolrua keeps them all in one place, organized by category, and usable without creating an account.

How it works

Most tools on Toolrua run entirely in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly, so your files are processed on your own device rather than sent to a remote server. A small number of tools (currently the PDF Password Protector) process files on our server because the underlying library requires it — those cases are called out explicitly in our Privacy Policy, and any uploaded file is deleted immediately after processing.

Why it's free

Toolrua is supported by advertising rather than subscriptions or paywalls. Keeping tools free and ad-supported means anyone can use them without friction, while the ads on the site help cover hosting and ongoing development.

Our approach

  • No sign-up. Every tool works the moment you land on the page.
  • Privacy-first where possible. Client-side processing means your files typically never reach a server.
  • Always improving. New tools and fixes ship regularly based on what people actually need.
